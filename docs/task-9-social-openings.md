# Task #9 — Social / Openings Marketing

GoBookr's first social marketing release is intentionally phone-first and does not pretend to publish directly into a professional's social account.

## Professional flow

1. Open Marketing and choose **Openings Today**.
2. Enter real appointment times and optional Story copy/photo.
3. Publish the openings to GoBookr so customers can discover the same-day availability.
4. Prepare a 1080 × 1920 branded Story image.
5. Tap **Share to socials** on a supported phone to open the native share sheet, or save the image as a fallback.
6. Copy the tracked GoBookr link and add it with the destination social app's link tool.
7. Mark openings filled when they are no longer available.

## Customer / measurement flow

- Published same-day openings appear on the GoBookr openings surface and the professional profile.
- Opening cards lead to a tracked GoBookr detail/booking path.
- Analytics measure opening-driven profile views and booking clicks as booking intent; they do not claim a completed appointment.

## Supported sharing

The UI provides destination-specific guidance for Instagram, Facebook, and Snapchat. The browser native share sheet chooses the actual installed destination. GoBookr does not store social credentials or claim that a Story was successfully published.

Automatic scheduled social posting is deliberately a later integration. The current release keeps scheduled campaign state separate from verified social delivery so GoBookr never reports a post as delivered when it cannot prove it.
