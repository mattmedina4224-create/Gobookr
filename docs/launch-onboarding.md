# GoBookr professional getting started

1. Search GoBookr for your name and workplace before creating a profile.
2. If your profile exists, use **Claim this profile**. Create an account or log in, provide evidence of ownership, and submit your request. A matching name alone is not ownership evidence.
3. Watch **Your profile claims** in your account dashboard for approval or rejection. A pending claim does not hide the public listing. If rejected, review the feedback and provide clearer evidence.
4. After approval, open your professional dashboard and finish your profile: accurate name, workplace, address, specialties and external booking URL. Upload only photos you own or have permission to use.
5. Open your public profile and test its booking link on your phone. Customers book through your external scheduling provider; GoBookr does not confirm that appointment.
6. Add openings in your marketing tools. Publishing openings makes them visible on GoBookr. Download Story images or copy campaign text and links to share manually.
7. Review your billing page. The professional plan is a 30-day trial followed by $20/month when you activate paid billing. A card is not automatically charged merely because an imported listing exists.

## Claim your profile

Find the listing with your current name and workplace. Select **Claim this profile**, sign up or log in, then submit a working booking-profile link or other meaningful ownership evidence. Do not submit government ID or sensitive personal information in free-text fields. An administrator reviews the request before control transfers. Approval keeps the existing listing rather than creating a duplicate, starts the professional trial and lets you edit the profile.

Repeated submissions from the same account do not create extra pending claims. Another person cannot take over a claimed profile through the claim form. For an incorrect listing or a rejected request, use the site's correction/reporting options or contact the GoBookr team.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are appointments booked?** Your existing scheduling provider. Check the external booking page for final availability, payment and cancellation rules.

**Is an unclaimed listing paid?** No. An attributed public-source listing can remain public before its owner claims it.

**What does the professional plan cost?** $20/month following a 30-day free trial. Paid checkout activates recurring billing. Without activation, an expired trial does not silently become a paid subscription. Checkout is unavailable during the last 48 hours of an active trial because of the current Stripe trial-end restriction; it becomes available after expiry.

**Can I cancel?** Use the billing page to manage an activated subscription. Review the cancellation date shown there. Provider-level trial cancellation and failed-payment tests are still pending a connected Stripe test account, so those scenarios are not launch-certified yet.

**Do business accounts pay?** GoBookr business accounts are complimentary. Their pages connect customers to staff and business booking options.

**Does GoBookr post automatically to Instagram or Facebook?** No connected automatic publishing integration has been confirmed. Scheduling saves a campaign to your queue; open it at the planned time, download/copy it and post manually.

**Do all profiles have a verified license?** No. A license badge is reserved for a license actually verified by GoBookr. Public-source attribution and ownership claims are separate from license verification.

**Can I use my phone?** Yes. Bookmark your professional dashboard for quick access to your profile, openings, marketing and billing. A native app is not required for launch.
