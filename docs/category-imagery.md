# Homepage category imagery

Three AI-generated general marketing images illustrate hairstylists, nail artists, and tattoo artists. They are never attached to named pros, fabricated reviews, or profile portfolios. Real profile photos continue to come from existing uploads.

The built-in image-generation tool produced the original square PNGs. Project versions are WebP assets, resized proportionately to 720 × 720 for the web, with no composition changes. Original outputs remain in the generating workspace. Combined optimized payload is under 150 KB; images lazy-load below the hero and reserve layout space.

- `public/images/category-nails-v1.webp`

## Prompt set

- Hair: candid editorial photograph of a hairstylist styling an adult client's soft brunette hair with caramel balayage, behind/side framing, bright white/beige contemporary salon with oak and natural daylight, realistic hands and hair texture. Square category crop; no words, logos, watermark, named real people, or collage.
- Nails: close editorial photograph of an adult nail technician painting an adult client's almond-shaped nails soft blush nude, realistic fingers, airy white/neutral salon with oak and daylight, natural skin texture, square framing; no words, logos, watermark, collage, or named provider/portfolio claims.
- Tattoos: adult tattoo artist's black-gloved hands using a modern tattoo pen on a delicate fine-line botanical forearm tattoo, clean bright neutral studio with oak/daylight, anatomically realistic hands/forearm, square framing; no blood, gore, branding, words, logos, watermark, collage, or named artist claims.

## Placement and behavior

A photo row sits between the existing homepage hero and the real-professional cards. It uses three desktop columns and horizontal swipe cards on narrow screens. Each photo links to the corresponding professional category, and “All specialties” opens the existing catalog. These versioned assets can be swapped independently for future visual experiments.
